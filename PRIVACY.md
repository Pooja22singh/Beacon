# Privacy

Beacon is a portfolio demo of a customer support chat agent. It is not a commercial service.

## What it collects

- Your Google account name, email address and Google user id, used only to sign you in.
- The messages you send in a chat, which are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a reply.
- Support tickets you ask the assistant to create.

## How it is used

- Only to run the demo. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.
- Chat history is kept in your browser during a session and is not stored on the server.
- A session cookie keeps you signed in for up to 7 days. Signing out clears it.

## Your choices

To have your data removed, open an issue on this repository and it will be deleted.
